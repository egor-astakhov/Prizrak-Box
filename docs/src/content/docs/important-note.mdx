Services provided by **Prizrak-Box** are intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service. If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

**VPN DATA**\
We **DO NOT** collect or store any VPN data.\
We **DO NOT** modify the contents of your VPN data.\
We **DO NOT** share any sensitive or user-specific data with third parties.

Prizrak-Box is a non-centralized VPN client where users connect to their own custom configured servers. This means you as a user connecting to your own server have complete control and liability over your VPN data. The app itself will not in any way modify any VPN data as it leaves the device to your configured VPN server.

**DATA COLLECTION**\
Prizrak-Box does not collect crash reports or analytical data.

By default, the app sends certain device information in HTTP headers when requesting subscription data (e.g., to fetch the list of servers):

- `X-Hwid` — a unique hardware device identifier (used if the server applies HWID Device Limit)
- `X-Device-OS` — operating system name
- `X-Ver-OS` — OS version
- `X-Device-Model` — device model

This information is used as a custom user identifier to support subscription functionality and connection management. Users can disable HWID transmission in the app settings. If disabled, the HWID will no longer be sent with requests to the server.

All profile data is stored locally on the device. No data is sent to any third-party service by the application itself.

If you install Prizrak-Box through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may share crash reports and usage data with the developer, but only if you have agreed to this in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers). This data is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the app itself does not collect or send it.

**PERMISSIONS**\
The app asks for the following permissions only when a feature needs them:

- Camera — to scan QR codes with profile links. Images are processed on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- Notifications — for subscription expiry and traffic reminders. They are scheduled locally on the device; no push server is used.
- VPN configuration — to start the VPN tunnel to the servers from your profile.

You can revoke any permission at any time in the device settings.

**ADVERTISING NETWORKS**\
Prizrak-Box does not use monetization and has no plans for it in the future.

**DATA RETENTION AND DELETION**\
All profiles, settings and logs are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time by removing profiles in the app or by deleting the app.

The device information listed above is sent only to the subscription server you add yourself. That server is operated by your subscription provider, not by Prizrak-Box, and it may keep this information according to its own policy. To have it deleted, contact your subscription provider. You can stop sending the HWID at any time in the app settings.

**CHILDREN'S PRIVACY**\
Prizrak-Box does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, whether child or adult. This means no personally identifiable information is collected from children under 13.
